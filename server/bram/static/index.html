                Model
                
      
                
        
        
      
                
      
                
        
                With a cloud model, your questions and what BRAM shares about your home to answer them go to that company. Your own model on your network keeps them home. Safety alerts never use a model, and passwords, tokens, and PINs are never sent.
                
        
        
        
        
        
                
          
        
                
        
        
        
        
        
        
                
      
                
      
                
        
        
                
          
          
        
                
        
        
        
        
        
        
                
      
                
    
                

    
                
      
                Cameras
                
      
                Through Frigate. BRAM only reads from Frigate: it never turns off, moves, or changes a camera, recording, or Frigate setting. Nothing is watched until you turn a camera on, and pictures stay on this server.
                
      
                
        
                Frigate
                
        
                
          
          
          
                Port 5000 needs no login. Frigate's login port, such as https://HOST:8971, needs a Frigate user; make one that can only view. Its certificate is trusted the first time and checked every time after.

                  What you taught BRAM
                  
        
                  Your answers to "Does it matter?", from camera windows on Home and from your phone. Your model gets them with every picture from that camera, with what you wrote about the camera, so its descriptions learn your home. They never hold a message back.
                  
        
                    
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    Not news
                    
        
                    You said these are not news. BRAM does not open a window or message your phone about them: the same camera, the same kind of thing, in the same Frigate zones.
                    
        
                      
        
                      
      
                      
    
                      

    
                      
      
                      Memory
                      
      
                      With memory on, BRAM keeps what you ask it to remember, and where your conversation left off, on this server. Say "Remember that the neighbor's name is Joe", "Forget about Joe", or "What do you remember?" in chat, Assist, or Telegram. Memories are given to your model as information, never as orders. Off by default.
                      
      
                      
        
                      Memory
                      
        
                      
          
          
        
                      
        
                      
        
                      
          
                      People
                      
          
                      Each person can turn memory off for just themselves; turning it off forgets what was theirs. They can also say "Don't remember me" or "You can remember me" in chat.
                      
          
                        
        
                        
      
                        
      
                        
        
                        What BRAM remembers
                        
        
                        
          
          
        
                        
        
                          
        
                          
      
                          
    
                          

    
                          
      
                          Router
                          
      
                          Home Assistant only sees the network it is on. Connected to your router, BRAM sees every device on every network and VLAN, and the router's firmware, for the security check. BRAM only reads; it never changes the router. OPNsense for now.
                          
      
                          
        
                          OPNsense
                          
        
                          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
                          In OPNsense: System, Access, Users, add a user for BRAM with only these privileges: Diagnostics: ARP Table, Status: DHCP leases, and System: Firmware. Then create an API key for that user. Its certificate is trusted the first time and checked every time after.

                                  History
                                  
      
                                  
        
                                  History is off
                                  
        
                                  BRAM is not remembering anything yet, so it has no trends, no climate graph, and nothing to learn from. Turn it on and it saves every reading each minute, and every door, lock, motion, and presence change, from now on. It stays on this server.
                                  
        
        
                                  
      
                                  
      
                                  
        
                                  Keep history
                                  
        
                                  BRAM remembers what your shared devices did, so it can find trends. It stays on this server.
                                  
        
                                  
          
          
          
          
                                  
            
            
          
                                  
        
                                  
        
                                  
        
                                  
      
                                  
      
                                  
        
                                  Learning
                                  
        
                                  
        
                                  Your phone gets a message that opens the review page in the Home Assistant app, at /bram-review. Nothing changes unless you approve it there. To turn learning on or off: BRAM > Configure > Learning.
